## 2. Executive Design Highlights

1. **Card-Based UI Architecture**: No plain text dumps. Every piece of information is structured inside elegant, rounded visual cards with subtle slate borders (`#E2E8F0`).
2. **Visual Numbering & Badges**: Process steps feature circular numbered badges (`01`, `02`, `03`...) in vibrant brand colors.
3. **Accent Top Strips**: Cards have colored top accent strips (Indigo, Cyan, Emerald, Amber) to visually categorize information at a glance.
4. **Hero Dark Slides for Bookends**: Slide 1 (Intro) and Slide 15 (Conclusion) use a deep executive navy theme (`#0F172A`) for strong visual impact.
5. **Human-Written Plain English**: Natural conversational wording throughout, with complete speaker notes included on every slide.
